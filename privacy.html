Privacy

Chess Strategy Trainer is free, open-source software with no ads and no tracking.

Without logging in

Everything stays in your browser: your scores, your games and their analysis. Nothing is sent to us.

When you log in with Lichess

Lichess tells us your username and nothing else: never your password or email. We keep, in your account:

  • your Lichess username;
  • your training: which positions you played, when, and your score;
  • the games you add (their moves, Lichess's or our engine's evaluations);
  • if you add it, your Chess.com username (your public Chess.com games are read through our server).

Your Lichess login stays in your browser. Our server keeps only a fingerprint of it, for a day, so it doesn't ask Lichess again on every request. Logging out removes it, and cancels our access on Lichess.

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare's servers, which run our account server and its database, and in your browser. We don't share or sell anything, and we use no analytics or advertising services.

Deleting your data

In the app: Your profile › Delete my account. Everything above is deleted at once, for good.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. The code that does all of this is there too.
