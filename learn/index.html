Chess strategy training: the exercises

Tactics puzzles always have one right answer. Real games don't. ChessStrategy.org trains what comes before the move: reading the position, choosing a plan, finding good moves, and picking the best one.

Imbalance readingChess imbalances: learn to read a positionPlan finderHow to find a plan in chessCandidate movesCandidate moves in chess: find more than one good moveMove selectionHow to choose the best move in chess

Every exercise uses positions from master games, and from your own games once you connect Lichess or add your Chess.com username. Answers are checked with fixed counting rules and Stockfish, which runs in your browser. It's free, open source and has no ads.
